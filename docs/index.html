About the data

Source: TTC Subway Delay Data on the City of Toronto Open Data portal, 2019 through August 2026. Each record is a logged incident with a station, line, delay code and minutes of delay. The raw files were stacked into SQLite and cleaned in SQL (line names standardized, station names normalized, duplicate rows removed, delay codes joined to their descriptions and grouped into five categories).

Full years (2019 to 2025) are used for every chart except the yearly one, where 2026 is shown as a partial year. Delay hours are the sum of the "Min Delay" column. About 60% of logged incidents have zero delay minutes, so these are delay minutes actually recorded, not incident counts. The Scarborough RT closed in July 2023.
